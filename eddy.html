Our mascot

Meet Eddy

His full name is Edison, after Thomas Edison. His friends call him Eddy. He is built like an old-fashioned 5 mm LED, the same little LED that sits on our logo, and he has a job on just about every page of this site.

See his many looks Meet his creator
→

From logo to legend

Look just above the words Light Efficient Design. That blue and green shape is a classic 5 mm LED, the kind that lit up a generation of electronics. Give it a face, a personality and a job to do, and you get Eddy.

He is a small LED with a big sense of humor, and he helps out across all three of our brands: Light Efficient Design, RemPhos and Solera.

The artist behind Eddy

Brought to life by Amy George

Every Eddy on this page comes from Amy George, our Senior Creative Director. Amy has been with Light Efficient Design for more than 15 years, and she dresses Eddy up in so many fun ways that he now has a bigger wardrobe than most of us.

When you see Eddy holding a sample scoop, a magnifying glass or a water cannon, that is Amy's work. Thank you, Amy.
