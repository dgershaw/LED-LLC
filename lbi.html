LBI G1

Economical linkable white light bar. The economical LBI. Up to 146 lumens per watt, with wattage and color temperature you set on the bar. The economical Linkable Bar with Internal Driver, in the same 1.2 in profile and lengths as LBI G2. Three wattages and three color temperatures on the bar, in standard or warm models. A direct replacement for fluorescent strips, troffers, vapor tights, low and high bays, coves and stairwells.

Applications: Fluorescent strip replacement, Troffers and vapor tights, Low and high bays, Cove lighting, Stairwells.

LBI G1 Spec Sheet

LBI G2

Linkable white light bar. The everyday workhorse. Up to 160 lumens per watt, with wattage and color temperature you set on site. The new generation of the Linkable Bar with Internal Driver. A sleek, control-ready bar with a protective housing that links end to end into continuous runs, from offices and break rooms to retail aisles.

Applications: Offices, Break rooms, Retail aisles, Cove and display, Retrofit of existing fixtures.

LBI G2 Spec Sheet

LBI 65

Linkable white light bar, IP65 for wet locations. The same linkable system, sealed for wet and outdoor locations. Liquid-tight end caps keep the IP65 rating even with accessories. An IP65-rated linkable fixture or retrofit kit with a polycarbonate housing over an inner metal structure and heat sink. Built for parking garages, coolers, outdoor canopies, tunnels and walkways.

Applications: Parking garages, Coolers and freezers, Outdoor canopies, Tunnels and walkways, Food processing.

LBI 65 Spec Sheet

LBI MAX

Linkable strip fixture, high lumens for higher ceilings. More light from the same linkable system. Up to 6,365 lumens from a 4 ft strip, with corner pieces for seamless 90° runs. A linkable linear strip fixture or retrofit kit for commercial, retail, manufacturing, warehouse and display applications. Wattage and color temperature are set with switches inside the unit.

Applications: Warehouses, Manufacturing, Retail, Grocery and display, High ceilings.

LBI MAX Spec Sheet

LBI Color Palette

Linkable precision wavelength selection light bar. Six precise colors on the bar you already know. Pick the wavelength the job calls for and switch between two colors on site. Our linkable bar with an internal driver, delivering precision wavelength selection. Each bar carries two colors on a switch: red or orange, amber or green, blue or violet. It uses the same accessories as the regular LBI.

Applications: Labs and clean rooms, Venues and stages, Aircraft cabins, Signaling and safety, Displays, Wellness spaces.

LBI Color Palette Sell Sheet

LBI Color Palette +

Linkable RGBWW light bar: white light and limitless color in one. White light out of the box. Any color from the remote. One bar does both. LBI Color Palette + is an LBI G2 that can also mix limitless color. Every bar ships set to white light, with FlexWatt and FlexColor switches identical to LBI G2, so it installs, dims and qualifies for rebates like any white bar. Add the optional RGBWW remote and the same bar becomes a full color tunable fixture. RGBWW means red, green, blue, warm white and cool white LEDs in one bar: any color, and any white from 2700K to 5000K.

Applications: Theaters and cinemas, Entertainment venues, Bars and restaurants, Bridges and facades, Retail displays, Houses of worship.

LBI Color Palette X

Linkable RGBWW color tunable light bar with DMX. Full RGBWW color under DMX control. Program scenes, cues and shows from the console your venue already runs. A linkable RGBWW light bar driven over DMX. Red, green, blue, warm white and cool white each have their own channel, so one bar mixes any color and any white from the console, for venues and installations that need programmed scenes and precise control of every run. Full specifications will be added when the spec sheet is ready.

Applications: Venues and stages, Architectural facades, Theme and attractions, Broadcast, Arenas.

LBI 65 Color Palette +

IP65 linkable precision wavelength light bar, set by remote. The LBI Color Palette, sealed for wet locations. The same six colors, set with the included remote. An IP65-rated linkable LED bar with an internal driver, delivering precision wavelength selection. Liquid-tight end caps and the same seamless, vapor-tight linking as the LBI 65. There are no switches on the bar: each bar carries two colors and four wattages, chosen with the included remote.

Applications: Venues, Oceanfronts, Signaling, High visibility, Wet and outdoor areas.
