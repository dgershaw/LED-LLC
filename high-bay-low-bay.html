Light Efficient Design · Screw-In Retrofits

High Bay + Low Bay

Replaces up to 1000W HID

Ultra-bright LED retrofits for high ceiling environments. Models without active cooling for open fixtures & silent operation. Models with active cooling are safe to install in enclosed fixtures. Low bay retrofits are ideal for ceiling fixtures that require light to be pushed down and out. Angled LED panels help direct light exactly where it is needed.
