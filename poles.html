More details

How to read a pole part number

SL-RSP = round straight pole, SL-RPSQ = square straight pole; the following segments are mounting height in feet, shaft diameter in inches and wall thickness gauge (11 or 7); AB = anchor base, EMB = direct burial (embedded); XX = mounting option (T2 / T2.5 / T3 tenon); YY = finish color (BK / DB / GR). Example: SL-RSP-20-4-11-AB-T2-DB.
