Photos

Photo controls are presets a person picks; nothing moves on its own. Main product photos always show the whole product.

{% for name, ar in [("Square", "1"), ("4:3", "4/3"), ("3:2", "3/2"), ("16:9 wide", "16/9"), ("21:9 panorama", "21/9"), ("3:4 tall", "3/4")] %} {% endfor %}
  • Fit: fill the frame (may crop the edges) or show the whole photo.
  • Photo focus: which part stays in view when a photo is cropped or zoomed, for example 50% 30% (across, down).
  • Zoom: 100 % to 300 %, from the photo focus.
  • Width and side: photo blocks, diagrams and banners; on phones they always fill the width.

Fixed on every page

Set in the site's templates, so they hold whatever is edited.

  • Brand and product names are written in full: Light Efficient Design, RemPhos, Solera, LBI G1, LBI G2, LBI 65, LBI MAX, LBI Palette, LBI Palette+, LBI Palette DMX, LBI 65 Palette+.
  • Logos are used exactly as supplied, only scaled.
  • High contrast everywhere and no text under 12 px.
  • Product photos at the top show the whole product. Specifications, part numbers, documents and Next steps stay in their place at the end of each product page.
  • Every page works on a laptop and on a phone; on phones, side-by-side blocks stack.
