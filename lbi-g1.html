RemPhos · Linkable Light Bars

Linkable Bar / Internal Driver (LBI G1)

The economical LBI, up to 146 lumens per watt.

  • Up to 146 lumens per watt, the economical replacement for fluorescent lamps
  • Link 33 four-foot bars together at 120VAC, or 40 at 277VAC
  • Replaces linear and general purpose strips, troffers, vapor tight fixtures, low and high bays, cove lighting and stairwells
  • Many accessories let the LBI mount in a wide variety of applications
  • Your choice of Simple & Networked Lighting Controls
