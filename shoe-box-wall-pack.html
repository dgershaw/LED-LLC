Case study

Pic Quik · Las Cruces, NM

Top-to-bottom store retrofit

Gas station, convenience store and car wash

28stores in the chain now being retrofitted

Situation
HID gas canopy fixtures were wasting energy and caused a maintenance headache, with bulbs and ballasts constantly being replaced.
Solution
The gas canopy fixtures were easily retrofitted with the directional, horizontal-mount LED-8090. Ease of install and superior light output immediately led to retrofitting the entire store.
Result
A brighter, more efficient retail environment that is safer and easier to shop, all while standing out amongst the competition.

“The overall look of the store and light output after being retrofitted was so impressive… I told my managers to do every location ASAP.”

Owner, Pic Quik (28 locations)
