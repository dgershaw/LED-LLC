More details

Vapor-tight bundles — one SKU solution

Each bundle is a single SKU combining the LBI65 bar in a 4 ft (25W) or 8 ft (50W) slim vapor-tight configuration, optionally with a PIR occupancy sensor (-OCC) or a Bluetooth PIR sensor (-OCCBT). Seamless connector(s) and an incoming power conduit adapter are included with every bundle.

Linking

Waterproof linking: connect up to 30 4 ft units together @ 120VAC or 40 4 ft units @ 277VAC.
