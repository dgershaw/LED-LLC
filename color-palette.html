More details

LBI Color PaletteX with DMX

DMX is stepping up to the light bar. More control. More possibilities.

RGBWW remote

The optional RP-REMOTE-RGB-WC remote (holster included) adjusts every LBI Color Palette+ bar in range: white presets, full RGBWW color, dimming and scenes. Try it in the LBI Family Overview.

LBI Color Palette+ default mode and on-bar switches

LBI Color Palette+ ships in white light mode and carries G2-style on-bar switches (FlexWatt wattage and FlexColor CCT, as on the standard RP-LBI-G2 bar). Color is selected with the optional RP-REMOTE-RGB-WC remote; one remote adjusts every bar in range.
