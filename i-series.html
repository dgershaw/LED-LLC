Solera i-⁠Series · Modular solar lighting with Solera Connect

One pole. Every piece chosen for the job.

i-⁠Series is a solar lighting platform: pick the light head, the power scheme, the solar-and-battery pack, the optics and the pole, and every fixture reports in over 4G. Configure the exact system below, or answer a few questions and let the page recommend one.

How the platform fits together

Six parts, each chosen for the site. The battery box, controller and 4G module ride the pole below the panels, so nothing is buried or trenched.

Light head

A1 Standard (50 W, up to 10,000 lm), A2 High Output (100 W, up to 15,000 lm) or the D1 Decorative gooseneck (20 W, up to 3,000 lm). 160+ lm/W, IP65, L70 100,000 hours.

Solar panels

Two 55 W panels or one 200 W panel, monocrystalline 12 V, with adjustable tilt and rotation. Face them south and tilt about 45°.

Battery and controller

600 Wh or 1,200 Wh LiFePO4 with an MPPT charge controller and battery management. About 6 hours of full sun to charge; about 30 hours of light on a charge.

Solera Connect

A 4G LTE module in every fixture. Status, history, schedules, alarms and energy reports from lampmonitor.com or the app, set up by Solera before it ships.

Programs

Ships at 80 % for two hours, then motion mode at 20 % with 80 % on movement. Change it to full power or all-night constant from the app.

Pole

20, 25 or 30 ft square straight steel, anchor base or direct burial, rated by EPA for 80 to 120 mph winds. The configurator checks the pole against the fixture.

Answer what you know about the site. The page proposes a head, a pack, optics, a program and a pole, and explains why. A free photometric layout from Solera confirms spacing and light levels.

Solera Connect: it's not just lighting

Visibility, control and data for every pole, from the web portal at lampmonitor.com or the iOS and Android apps. Solera commissions each fixture before it ships; you open the app and see your project.

  • See every pole on a map, by project, group or zone, with lamp, charge, battery and solar history.
  • Schedule it with lighting profiles: dim, motion and all-night programs by time or season.
  • Know first: alarms open work orders, so a fault is a ticket, not a dark lot.
  • Report it: energy and emissions reports export to Excel for owners and grant files.
  • Carrier-agnostic 4G LTE, one connection per fixture, included in the part number.

Poles rated for your configuration

Solera's recommended i-⁠Series poles with their maximum EPA at each wind speed. A check mark means the pole carries the configured fixture at that wind; the pole you picked above is highlighted.

EPA (effective projected area, sq ft) from the Solera Pole Offering. The A2 head needs a 4 in tenon (T4) or a 2-3/8 to 4 in adapter; A1 and D1 use the 2-3/8 in tenon (T2). Anchor-base poles come with an 11 or 12 in base plate and 1 × 40 in anchor bolts; direct-burial poles set 4 to 5 ft deep. Poles are made to order and are not returnable. The 30 ft direct-burial ratings are as printed in the offering.
