Solera i-⁠Series · Intelligent modular solar lighting

One pole. Every piece chosen for the job.

Choose the light head, power, solar-and-battery pack, optics and pole for your site. Every fixture is monitored and adjusted over 4G with Solera Connect.

A professional-grade, solar-powered lighting system for every season.

Solera Solar Lighting is the new standard for solar light performance. You can expect a professional-grade, configurable, solar-powered lighting system that provides bright lighting where you want it. With the i-⁠Series line, you can build and remotely program, control and monitor an intelligent solar lighting network. Graphically visualize usage and demand to make the most of your solar lighting.

Solera Connect · 4G in every fixture

Commissioned, monitored and adjusted from anywhere.

A carrier-agnostic 4G LTE module in each i-⁠Series links every pole to the Solera Connect web portal and app.

  • Commissioned remotelySolera sets up each fixture and your project in Connect before it ships.
  • Reports its healthBattery, charging, solar and lamp data for every pole, on a map or a list, with alarms that open work orders.
  • Adjusted remotelyChange schedules and lighting profiles, or switch lights on and off, for one pole, a group or the whole project.
