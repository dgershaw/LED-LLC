What the gondola holds

The gondola renders show boxed Bollard Retrofit and Post Top Retrofit lamps (3 ft gondola: one shelf of Bollard Retrofit then two of Post Top Retrofit; 2 ft gondola: two shelves of Bollard Retrofit then two of Post Top Retrofit); the 3 ft gondola's bottom shelf shows six LED Work Site String Light boxes. Boxes are illustrative merchandising; products are ordered separately.
